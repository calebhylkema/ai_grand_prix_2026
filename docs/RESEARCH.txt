## 2. Technical Approach — Our Stack

### Pipeline
```
Camera Frame → Gate Detection → PnP Pose → ESKF Fusion → MPCC Planner → Controller → Throttle/Roll/Pitch/Yaw
```

### Design Philosophy (from ChatGPT research + our additions)
1. Deterministic infrastructure first
2. Latency is performance (<40ms total pipeline target)
3. Adapter boundary protects core autonomy from sim API changes
4. Finish-rate baseline always exists (reliability > speed)
5. Aggressive mode layered on top of conservative baseline
6. Exploit telemetry fully in virtual phase
7. Remain monocular-only capable for physical stage

## 3. Gate Detection — State of the Art

### Approaches Ranked by Effectiveness
1. **YOLOv8 keypoint detection** — ~99% accuracy, ~20ms inference. Directly predicts corner coordinates. Best tradeoff for our needs.
2. **U-Net segmentation + QuAdGate corner extraction** — Used by MonoRace (2026 A2RL winner). Higher precision corners but more complex pipeline.
3. **Classical CV** (HSV threshold + contour + quad fitting) — ~34.6% accuracy in general, but sufficient for VQ1's simplified environment with highlighted gates. <5ms latency.

### PnP Pose Estimation
- OpenCV `solvePnP` with known 3D gate geometry (e.g., 1.5m × 1.5m square)
- Needs minimum 4 non-collinear 2D-3D point correspondences
- Most reliable at 2–5m distance with 6+ detected corners (MonoRace finding)
- At far distances: small rotation errors → large position errors
- At close range: motion blur degrades corner detection
- MonoRace solution: use "de-rotated method" (EKF attitude + PnP translation only) when full PnP is unreliable

### Newest Advance: ADR-VINS (March 2026)
- Tightly-coupled ESKF that integrates gate corner reprojection errors directly
- Eliminates intermediate PnP solver
- Works with as few as 2 visible corners (vs 4 for standard PnP)
- Demonstrated up to 20.9 m/s at A2RL Season 2
- Paper: arXiv:2603.02742

### Our Strategy
- **VQ1:** Classical CV (HSV + contour). Fast, simple, sufficient for highlighted gates.
- **VQ2+:** YOLOv8-nano keypoint detector, ONNX export with TensorRT optimization.
- **Dual-mode:** Auto-detect environment and switch backends.

## 4. State Estimation — ESKF Architecture

### Why ESKF (Error-State Kalman Filter)
- Standard proven architecture for monocular drone racing
- Handles the asynchronous nature of IMU (200+ Hz) and vision (20–60 Hz) naturally
- Better numerical stability than standard EKF for quaternion orientation

### State Vector (16D)
```
x = [position(3), velocity(3), orientation_quat(4), accel_bias(3), gyro_bias(3)]
```

### Key Implementation Details
- IMU propagation at 200+ Hz (prediction step)
- Gate-based PnP corrections at camera framerate (correction step)
- **Latency compensation is critical:** At 100 km/h, 10ms delay = 0.28m error. Buffer IMU measurements, propagate forward from measurement timestamp to current time.
- Chi-squared gating to reject outlier measurements
- Use ALL visible gates for updates (not just next target gate) — key AlphaPilot insight
- Handle IMU saturation (>16g) with model-predicted fallback

### Measurement Update Tiers
- **High confidence** (6+ corners, 2–5m range): Full 6-DOF update
- **Medium confidence**: Position-only update with de-rotated method
- **Low confidence** (<2 corners or >10m): Skip update, IMU-only propagation

## 5. Planning — MPCC and Alternatives

### MPCC (Model Predictive Contouring Control)
- Developed by Romero, Foehn, Scaramuzza at UZH (IEEE T-RO 2022)
- Combines trajectory generation and tracking in single optimization
- Maximizes progress along geometric 3D path while minimizing contouring error
- Achieved 60 km/h with >5g accelerations, outperforming human pilots
- **MPCC++** (RSS 2024): Adds safety constraints, residual aero models, Bayesian tuning → 100% gate success rate matching RL lap times

### Racing Line Generation
- Fit cubic B-spline through gate centers
- Curvature-based speed profile (slow in turns, fast on straights)
- CPC (Complementary Progress Constraints) for theoretically time-optimal trajectories (offline, minutes-to-hours compute)

### MPCC Formulation
```
minimize: -w_progress * θ_dot + w_contour * e_c² + w_lag * e_l² + w_input * ||u||²
subject to: quadrotor dynamics, thrust/rate limits, gate tunnel constraints, progress monotonicity
```
- Solver: CasADi + acados for real-time NMPC
- Horizon: 20–40 steps at 50ms intervals = 1–2s lookahead
- Target solve time: <20ms

### Key Papers
- MPCC original: arXiv:2108.13205 / IEEE T-RO 2022
- MPCC++: arXiv:2403.17551 / RSS 2024
- CPC time-optimal trajectories: Science Robotics 2021

## 6. Control — RL as Performance Ceiling

### Deep RL Approach (Swift, Nature 2023)
- PPO-trained policies with teacher-student distillation
- Teacher trains with privileged state → student learns via imitation → fine-tune with constrained RL
- Achieved 40ms sensorimotor latency (vs 220ms human)
- Won 15/25 races against 3 world champions
- **Key limitation:** Failed when conditions differed from training. Humans more adaptable.

### G&CNet (MonoRace, 2026 A2RL Winner)
- Tiny 3×64-neuron MLP: state → motor commands at 500 Hz
- Small enough for microcontroller deployment
- Bypasses traditional cascaded PID entirely
- Trained with RL in simulation

### Our Strategy
- **Primary:** MPCC (interpretable, safety guarantees, no training required)
- **Parallel:** RL policy development (PPO, G&CNet architecture)
- **Fallback ladder:** MPCC → PID cascade → hover/brake → emergency stop
- Use MPCC as reliable baseline that completes courses; layer RL on top for speed

### Why Not RL-Only?
- Reliability wins championships (AlphaPilot lesson)
- RL requires extensive sim training before deployment
- MPCC gives us a competing entry from day 1 when sim drops
- RL policy can be hot-swapped in once validated

## 7. Lessons from Prior Competitions

### AlphaPilot (Lockheed Martin, 2019)
- $1M prize. Team MAVLab (TU Delft) won with 12-second championship time
- **Key lesson: Reliability > speed.** MAVLab barely flew in first race but improved dramatically
- All teams used identical DRL RacerAI drones with NVIDIA Jetson AGX Xavier
- UZH RPG innovation: use ANY visible gate for localization, not just next target

### Swift (UZH, Nature August 2023)
- First autonomous system to beat human world champions in physical drone racing
- Won 15/25 races against Vanover, Bitmatta, Schaepper
- 0.5s faster best lap, primarily from 40ms vs 220ms reaction time
- Trained entirely in simulation (~1 month simulated flight = <1 hour desktop)
- **Key limitation:** Brittle to unexpected conditions (lighting changes, etc.)

### MonoRace (TU Delft MAVLab, A2RL Championship January 2026)
- **Most relevant precedent for AI Grand Prix** — same sensor suite (monocular + IMU)
- Won with monocular-only perception, beat 3 human world champion FPV pilots
- Peak velocity 28.23 m/s (~100 km/h)
- G&CNet control policy at 500 Hz on microcontroller
- U-Net GateSeg + QuAdGate corner extraction + ESKF + RL controller
- Paper: arXiv:2601.15222

### A2RL Drone Racing Challenge (2025–2026)
- Abu Dhabi Autonomous Racing League, partnered with DCL
- Multiple seasons with NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX onboard compute
- ADR-VINS (3rd place): OpenVINS-based VIO with YOLO drift corrections (arXiv:2512.20475)
- Tightly-coupled ESKF approach (arXiv:2603.02742): direct corner reprojection in filter

### Game of Drones (NeurIPS 2019)
- Microsoft AirSim-based competition
- Reinforcement learning and classical approaches competed

### Evolution of Winning Speeds
- 2016–2017: Classical CV + waypoint tracking → ~0.6 m/s
- 2018–2019: Deep learning detection + model planning → ~8 m/s (AlphaPilot)
- 2020–2022: Time-optimal planning (CPC, MPCC) + MPC → ~17 m/s
- 2023: Deep RL beating humans (Swift) → ~22 m/s with stereo
- 2024–2026: Monocular + neural controllers (MonoRace) → ~28 m/s

## 8. Latency Budget

At 100 km/h, every millisecond costs 0.028m of positional accuracy.

| Stage | Target | Hard Limit |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Camera capture | 1–5ms | 5ms |
| Neural inference | 10–17ms | 20ms |
| PnP + ESKF update | 1–3ms | 5ms |
| MPCC solve | 10–20ms | 25ms |
| Command generation | <1ms | 2ms |
| **Total pipeline** | **<40ms** | **57ms** |

Swift achieved 40ms total. MonoRace's G&CNet runs at 500 Hz (2ms) but perception adds ~17ms.

TensorRT optimization reduces inference latency 2–3× on Jetson hardware.
